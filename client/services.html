Our Services

From full RV repairs and roof maintenance to annual water heater and furnace service — we handle it all at your location throughout Sullivan, Orange & Ulster Counties.

What We Offer

Comprehensive RV Care

We come to you with the tools and expertise to diagnose and fix whatever your RV needs — no tow required.

RV Repair

We can diagnose and fix any issue you may have with your RV. Common areas we service:

  • Appliances
  • Leveling Jacks
  • Awnings
  • Electrical
  • Plumbing
  • AC Units
  • Roof

Roof Maintenance

Protect your investment from the top down. Our roof maintenance service keeps leaks away before they start.

  • Check for cracks or tears in membrane
  • Re-chalk problem areas on cap
  • Seal around vents, skylights & fans

RV Orientation

Many people buy an RV and don't fully understand how to use all its systems or what maintenance schedules to follow. We walk you through your unit, explain how everything works, and set you up for success as an RV owner.

Water Heater Service

Recommended annually for safe, reliable operation. Our thorough inspection covers:

  • Disinfect the system
  • Check anode rod condition
  • Test & inspect relief valve
  • Check complete operation
  • Inspect burner assembly
  • Check for nests, bugs & debris

Furnace Service

Keep your RV's heating system running safely and efficiently. Our furnace service includes:

  • Clean burner orifice
  • Clean blower wheel
  • Inspect control board
  • Clean & inspect combustion chamber
  • Inspect fan motor
  • Inspect duct work

Don't See Your Issue?

If it's on your RV, chances are we can fix it. Give us a call and describe what's going on — we'll let you know if we can help and get you scheduled fast.
